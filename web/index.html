How Analog Finder Works

Reproducible Quantitative Geomorphometry Pipeline

1

Standardized Spatial Scale (~10 km Blocks)

Terrain slope, roughness, and local relief are scale-dependent fractal properties. To prevent sensor resolution bias, Earth (NOAA ETOPO 2022), Mars (MGS MOLA), and Moon (LRO LOLA) are processed at uniform ~10 km spatial windows.

2

Z-Score Normalization & Covariance De-correlation

Every feature is standardized using Earth's continental background distribution: \(z = (x - \mu)/\sigma\). Mahalanobis distance automatically de-correlates the 98.7% collinearity between roughness and relief, isolating true morphological shapes.

3

Dual Similarity Engine & Real-Time Re-weighting

Users can explore both directional alignment (Weighted Cosine Similarity) and multivariate distance (Mahalanobis Distance), with live client-side re-ranking across 2,500 candidate terrain cells.

4

Honest Scientific Limitations

Atmospheric thermal variations between vacuum (Moon), thin \(CO_2\) (Mars), and Earth greenhouse dynamics prevent direct temperature matching without distortion. This pipeline emphasizes rigorous geomorphometry and macroclimate aridity.
